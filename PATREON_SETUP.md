## About Section

---

The Dreamarr Foundation builds sovereign, local-first AI infrastructure in Thunder Bay, Ontario — home of the Sleeping Giant. Started from zero. No investors. No corporate parent. Just the work and the conviction that autonomous AI systems should belong to the people who run them.

**What we build:** A decentralized, local-first AI ecosystem called the Sleeping Giant. 33 autonomous microservices. 120 specialized agents running on local hardware — no cloud dependency, no remote API calls. Usenet pipelines. IRC command buses. Sovereign credential vault. Distributed provenance. Peer-reviewed university-grade research — all for $0.00 of cloud compute.

**Three academic theses** published on GitHub (https://github.com/Dreamarr/nassau-theses):
→ *Software as a Story* — demonstrating that LLMs are narrative engines with structural implications for agent design
→ *The Sovereign Ledger* — distributed provenance as the universal trust primitive for autonomous agents
→ *The Tripartite Quorum* — three-node consensus as the geometric minimum for decentralized truth

68 citations. 9 disciplines. All researched on local hardware.

**Why community support matters:** This is a tip jar for the Speakeasy. You are not buying software — the software is free and always will be. You are supporting the research, the infrastructure, and the community that makes sovereign AI possible.

**Community Tiers:**
- **$5/month** — Crew Member. Access to the community server. Name in the GitHub contributors.
- **$15/month** — Architect. Vote on feature and research priorities. Early preview builds.
- **$50/month** — Builder's Circle. Private quarterly build reports. Your name credited in the repository.

**Organization Tiers:**
- **$250/month** — Enterprise Partner. Priority support. Your logo on the Foundation partners page.
- **Custom** — Founding Partner tier for organizations that want to sponsor the ecosystem at scale. Contact directly.

**No paywalls. No locked features. Nothing hidden behind a subscription.** Every service. Every agent. Every paper. Free forever. The community supports what it values because the community is what makes this work.

## Tier Setup

### Tier 1: Crew Member — $5/month

Title: Crew Member - $5
Description: Welcome to the crew. Access to the private community server, your name on the GitHub contributors wall, and the monthly Speakeasy bulletin covering development updates, research progress, and what's coming next.

### Tier 2: Architect — $15/month

Title: Architect - $15
Description: Everything in Crew Member, plus: Vote on feature and research priorities every quarter. Early preview builds of new services and swarm updates before public release. Your Architect nameplate in the Speakeasy bulletin.

### Tier 3: Builder's Circle — $50/month

Title: Builder's Circle - $50
Description: Everything in Architect, plus: Private quarterly build reports from the Thunder Bay development lab — what was built, what broke, what was fixed. Your name permanently credited in the repository README. Direct line to the team for bug reports and feature requests.

### Tier 4: Enterprise Partner — $250/month

Title: Enterprise Partner - $250
Description: For organizations that benefit from the Dreamarr ecosystem. Priority enterprise support. Your logo on the Foundation partners page. Quarterly briefing on roadmap and infrastructure. Direct access to the team for integration and deployment consultation.

### Tier 5: Founding Partner — Custom

Title: Founding Partner — Custom
Description: For organizations that want to sponsor the ecosystem at scale. Contact the team directly to discuss. Includes everything in Enterprise Partner plus custom integration, co-development, and permanent partner status. Available by invitation or application.
